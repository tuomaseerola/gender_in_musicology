
Subject area: Gender in peer-reviewed articles of music scholarship
Approach: Database
I am conducting a project on how gender shapes music research, using published journal articles as evidence. For this, I have compiled a database of 3,513 articles published over the last 25 years in musicology, ethnomusicology, and popular music studies, drawn from 15 leading journals.
The database already contains detailed information, including DOIs for every article, but in some cases the Scopus source data is missing the authors’ affiliation (country). In addition, the gender attributions of authors should be double-checked.

Your task would be to help complete and refine this dataset by:
- Adding missing author affiliations (country)
- Verifying the accuracy of gender attributions

The database is straightforward to use and easy to share (I share it online with permissions for you to edit with clear instruction). Once we complete the missing information, the database opens up exciting possibilities: we can explore gender distributions across different subfields of music research, and trace geographic and temporal trends in authorship roles across sub-disciplines of music research and consider questions of fairness and gender equity in these disciplines as well. From geographical data (country of the affiliation) we can also consider how anglo-american emphasis do the disciplines have and how these trends might have changed over time and ask many other questions that cannot be answered in other ways.
